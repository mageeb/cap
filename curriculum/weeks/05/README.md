# Week 5: Product Discovery — From a Demo to a PRD

Use an AI-assisted translation review application to practice defining a business product before implementing it. Move from a working desktop demonstration to users, jobs, a bounded MVP, measurable acceptance criteria, and web UX.

## What you will produce

A reviewed PRD, an explicit MVP boundary, annotated wireframes, and a record of decisions and open questions. This week does not require implementation, a chosen stack, a database migration, or a deployed application.

The classroom case study turns an Arabic-to-English machine draft into a human-reviewed translation. Its eventual vision includes multiple reviewers, adjudication, publishing, and a trusted parallel corpus. The initial exercise concentrates on one reviewer working through one small document.

Each student develops their own product artifacts for this case study. Brainstorm together, then defend your own scope and UX. Earlier projects can continue independently; this exercise does not replace every student's previously approved capstone domain.

## Delivery and preparation

The slides support a 75-minute discussion based on the recorded session. The outline expands it into the course's three-hour workshop with a 45-minute discovery demonstration and 60 minutes of student practice. These are prepared teaching materials, not evidence of a rehearsed software demonstration.

Bring your existing AI assistant, a document editor, and a way to sketch screens. No new subscription, framework, or API key is required. If the desktop prototype is available, demonstrate it; otherwise use the walkthrough in the session notes and label sketches as illustrative.
